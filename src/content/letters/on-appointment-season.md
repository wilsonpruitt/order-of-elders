Friends,

Appointment season is not gentle to anyone, moving or staying. If you're packing boxes, I am praying for a soft landing and a first casserole that actually gets delivered. If you're staying and receiving someone new, I am praying you make that landing soft.

The itinerancy asks something of us that most vocations don't — that we hold our place with an open hand. That is a spiritual discipline, not just an administrative fact, and it is easier to carry inside an order that notices.

Grace and peace,
The Chair
