Order,

Fifty days is longer than forty, and I think that is on purpose. Lent trains us to sit with hard things for a set season; Eastertide asks something harder — to keep proclaiming resurrection after the lilies are gone and the sanctuary looks ordinary again.

Mark your calendar now for the fall retreat. I'd rather you hear about it in April with time to plan than in August as one more thing landing on you.

Grace and peace,
The Chair
