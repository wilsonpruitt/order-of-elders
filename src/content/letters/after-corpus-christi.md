Sisters and brothers,

I am still carrying the ordination service with me — the historic questions asked aloud, the hands laid on, the whole room answering for those being set apart. It is a strange and good thing that our Discipline still asks us to do this in public, together, rather than in private paperwork.

Wherever you served this year, whatever this conference cost you, thank you for the ministry of Word, Sacrament, Order, and Service you carried into it. Watch over one another in love this summer.

Grace and peace,
The Chair
