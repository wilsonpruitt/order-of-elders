> "But immediately Jesus spoke to them and said, "Take heart, it is I; do not be afraid."
>
> — Matthew 14:27

The thing about translating the scriptures is that sometimes I do it and sometimes I don’t. Sometimes, translation causes me to take a rabbit trail to nowhere. Seriously, my ADHD brain can spend hours on one word, and I feel like I’m showing off my nerdier side when I talk about Greek and Hebrew in my sermons. I don’t think anyone actually cares that I can translate. I’m certainly no scholar. I just took a class and I use Accordance well. My congregation cares more about how I show up for them than my homework. So sometimes, I rely on good translations and just read on.
And sometimes, when I do, I miss surprises like this. Did you know “It is I” is ego eimi?” No predicate. Just *ego eimi*. I don’t expect *ego eimi* in Matthew. It’s everywhere in John. but not here. Right here? In the midst of this crazy, demon-like storm, there’s *ego eimi*. After feeding 5000, after rowing and fighting a storm for hours, when their arms are weak and shaking, when their backs feel broken, rain stinging their faces, hair drenched and in their eyes, in their tired, weariness, there’s *ego eimi*.
 Beloved, this week I needed to hear ego eimi. I needed a present God who walks toward me, who calls to me and calls me by name and the named one. Whatever else is happening in your life, *ego eim*i is here.
